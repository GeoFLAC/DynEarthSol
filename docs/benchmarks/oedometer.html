Elastoplastic oedometer test

An oedometer shortens a sample in one direction while its other sides cannot move. The strain is uniaxial, so the stress follows a closed-form path: elastic up to yield, then a straight plastic branch whose slope depends on the friction and dilation angles. This benchmark checks DES's Mohr–Coulomb return on that path.

At a glance
Dimension2D, plane strain
Buildmake ndims=2
Run timeabout 1 s
Inputsbenchmarks/mechanics/oedometer/oedometer-2d.cfg in the DynEarthSol repository
Checked withDynEarthSol cd1042c

Setup​

SettingValue
Cell1 m × 1 m, two triangles, plane strain (is_plane_strain = yes)
Loadingthe right side moves inward at 10−5 m/s; the left, top and bottom sides cannot move normal to themselves
ElasticityK=G=200 MPa
Strengthcohesion c = 1 MPa, friction angle ϕ = 10°, dilation angle ψ = 10°, no softening, tension cut-off 5.67 MPa
Otherno gravity; fixed time step 1 s, 2000 steps (20 mm of shortening)

With E1​=K+4G/3 and E2​=K−2G/3, the axial stress rises elastically as Δσxx​=E1​Δε and the lateral stresses as E2​Δε. With Nϕ​=(1+sinϕ)/(1−sinϕ), the cell yields at

∣σxx​∣=E1​−E2​Nϕ​2cNϕ​​E1​​,

here after 6.41 mm of shortening. Both lateral stresses are equal, so the return lies on the edge of two Mohr–Coulomb faces. With Nψ​=(1+sinψ)/(1−sinψ) the plastic branch has slope

dεd∣σxx​∣​=E1​+2r(E2​Nψ​−E1​),r=(E1​+E2​)Nϕ​Nψ​−2E2​(Nϕ​+Nψ​)+2E1​E1​−E2​Nϕ​​.

The reference evaluates this path at the true strain DES applies, ε=∑vΔt/(1−u) with u the shortening at the start of each step, because DES updates the cell's geometry every step.

Input and how to run​

cd benchmarks/mechanics/oedometer
../../../dynearthsol2d oedometer-2d.cfg

The run writes 51 frames, result.save.*, one every 40 steps. benchmarks/mechanics/oedometer/oedometer-2d-plot.py plots the shortening of the right side against σxx​ with a reference path; it was written for an older Matplotlib and needs small changes to run with a current one. Its path takes each step's strain at the end-of-step shortening and changes slope only at a whole step, which puts its final stress 0.03 % above the closed form.

What to look for​

DESReference
Axial stress after 20 mm7.0868 MPa7.0864 MPa
Largest relative difference, elastic branch0.017 %
Largest relative difference, plastic branch0.014 %

DES follows the path on both branches. The two elements carry the same stress, so the cell deforms uniformly, as the reference assumes.

Limits​

  • One stress path. Uniaxial strain tests the two-face edge return and the dilation angle, but not the apex or the tension cut-off.
  • No softening. Cohesion and friction stay constant; see the shear-band benchmark for softening.
