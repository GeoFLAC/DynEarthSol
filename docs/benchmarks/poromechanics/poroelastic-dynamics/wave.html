Undrained wave in a sealed column

A small initial velocity sets a sealed saturated column oscillating; compression raises the pressure and expansion lowers it.

Largest velocity error: 0.171% and largest pressure error: 0.262% of their amplitudes, over all nodes and 65 saved times.

At a glance
Dimension3D
Buildmake ndims=3 from the poromechanics source (not yet merged; DynEarthSol #134)
Inputswave.zip
Reproduce withDynEarthSol 3774df0

Problem setup​

Water and displacement boundaries. All water boundaries sealed; κ = 0 throughout. End vertical displacement and side-normal displacement fixed.

Initial disturbance. vz​(z,0)=Vsin[π(z+H)/H], V=10−6 m/s. Initially zero pressure, stress and strain.

Physical regime and comparison. Physical mixture density; no gravity, damping, heating, PT or GVS. Compare velocity and pressure over one period.

Input and how to run​

Every file this example needs is in wave.zip. Run from that folder:

# 3D. Restarts from the shipped checkpoint seed, frame 0: the column mesh with zero stress, strain and pressure and the initial sine velocity (listed in initial-velocity.csv). Runs 8192 explicit steps, one wave period, saving every 128th step as wave.* and writing a checkpoint at every saved frame.
dynearthsol3d input.cfg

Result: DES and the analytical solution​

(a) Vertical velocity at mid-height over one period, normalized by the initial amplitude V: line, eigenmode solution; symbols, DES saved frames. (b) Pore pressure at the sealed base over the same period, normalized by the pressure amplitude.

This checks fluid storage and elastic wave coupling together; local fluid content is conserved to round-off. It does not test a GVS earthquake cycle.

To reproduce the whole study behind this figure, from the DES source folder:

python3 benchmarks/poromechanics/undrained_wave.py dynearthsol3d OUT --spacing .0125 --steps 1024 2048 4096 8192 --samples 64 --restart-check
python3 benchmarks/poromechanics/undrained_wave.py dynearthsol3d OUT-h005 --spacing .05 --steps 4096 --samples 32
python3 benchmarks/poromechanics/undrained_wave.py dynearthsol3d OUT-h0025 --spacing .025 --steps 4096 --samples 32

Analytical reference​

S0​=ϕ/Kf​+(α−ϕ)/Ks​,Hu​=K+4G/3+α2/S0​,ρb​=(1−ϕ)ρs​+ϕρf​ cu​=Hu​/ρb​​,k=π/H,ω=kcu​,T=2H/cu​ vz​=Vsin[k(z+H)]cos(ωt),ϵzz​=(V/cu​)cos[k(z+H)]sin(ωt) p=−S0​cu​αV​cos[k(z+H)]sin(ωt),σzz​=Hu​ϵzz​

z is the DES coordinate, −H at the base and 0 at the top. V is the initial velocity amplitude, ρb​ physical mixture density, Hu​ the undrained constrained modulus and T the wave period. The selected material gives T≃1.162 ms.

Derivation​

Sealed local fluid content remains zero because it starts at zero. Eliminate pressure from the uniaxial effective-stress relation, giving an undrained modulus in the momentum equation.

ζ=αϵzz​+S0​p=0,σzz​=Hu​ϵzz​,ρb​utt​=Hu​uzz​

The fundamental sine mode vanishes at both fixed ends. Choose its amplitude to match the initial velocity V and differentiate displacement for strain; the zero-content relation then gives pressure.

uz​=(V/ω)sin[k(z+H)]sin(ωt)

Direct eigenmode solution of the stated sealed one-dimensional Biot wave problem; evaluated in undrained_wave.py.

Switching from explicit/GVS to physical inertia​

A separate restart control switches a nonzero coupled state after 32 accepted steps. The unit column has a fixed base, side-normal rollers, sealed water boundaries and a constant −1 kPa top traction. Internal Darcy flow remains active. Ordinary explicit and both GVS ceilings are checked at scalings 104, 105 and 106, in 2D/3D native and HDF5 output. Both phases use strain-driven pressure ordering; this does not convert a nonzero PT pending-pressure history.

The physical phase sets is_quasi_static=false, use_global_velocity_scaling=false and the neutral mass_scaling_reference_speed=shear. The independent mixture density is 2237.5 kg/m³. Minimum-simplex-altitude and undrained-wave calculations predict first physical intervals of 42.8985 µs (2D) and 35.0265 µs (3D). Every free velocity component checks the mass actually used:

vi,n+1​−vi,n​=ρb​Vi​Δt​Fi,n+1​.

Across the recorded 36 cases, the largest relative identity error is 4.23×10−16. Stress, strain, pressure and velocity are preserved at the switch; the reselected mass and timestep change deliberately. The closed Biot-content residual is at most 2.27×10−16 of its stored/deformation scale. A further physical restart preserves the continuation fields exactly.

At unchanged velocity, physical kinetic energy is continuous. Report the change in artificial kinetic energy separately:

ΔKnum​=21​i∑​(mi,physical​−mi,artificial​)∣vi​∣2.

The switch neither rescales velocity nor deposits that numerical energy change as heat. Full mechanical/thermal work, event-trigger hysteresis, RSF coseismic paths and complete earthquake-cycle transitions are outside this control. Reproduce it from the DES checkout with python3 tests/functional/check_inertia_switch.py ./dynearthsol2d OUT; repeat with the 3D executable and a separate output folder.

Validity and limits​

The standing-wave comparison uses linear small-strain, homogeneous, completely sealed, zero-permeability elasticity. The input file sets inertial_scaling, but with is_quasi_static = false DES uses the physical (unscaled) mass, so the value has no effect. This wave is a dynamic coupling check and does not establish earthquake-cycle or GVS equilibrium accuracy.
